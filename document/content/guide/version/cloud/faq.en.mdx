## Account and Login Issues

gptGO has two versions, and accounts are not shared between them:

China Mainland: <FastGPTLink campaign="docs_cloud_faq" content="login_help_cn" site="cn">{'https://github.com/zeroven0205-spec/'}</FastGPTLink> (supports WeChat and phone number login)

International: <FastGPTLink campaign="docs_cloud_faq" content="login_help_io" site="io">{'https://github.com/zeroven0205-spec/'}</FastGPTLink> (supports email, Google, and GitHub login; phone number sign-up was available before September 2024)

If you have used gptGO before but cannot log in, try switching between the two versions.

### Still Can't Log In? Contact Us

Contact an administrator in the Lark community.

## Basics

### Does gptGO Cloud Support Bringing Your Own Model?

gptGO Cloud currently does not support bringing your own model. You can only use the unified models provided by gptGO.
